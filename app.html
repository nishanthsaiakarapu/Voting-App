How to Evaluate a New Polling Station

Click any area above. A popup lets the officer change the maximum acceptable distance and expected voter-conversion rate, then compares nearby candidate buildings and estimates the constituency turnout increase.

Area Analysis
Estimate formula: additional voters = area non-voters × proportional distance reduction × expected conversion rate, capped by candidate-building capacity. Building distances/capacities are demo feasibility data and must be replaced with real location data for deployment.
This analysis uses aggregate non-voter patterns. It does not expose individual voting choices.
